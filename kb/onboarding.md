# Getting started

## Creating an account

Register on the sign-up page with your email address (use your university address), your full name, and a password of at least 8 characters. New accounts are student accounts with the default weekly quota and can book right away. Read the fair use and booking rules before your first booking.

## Finding hardware

### Browsing servers

The Servers page shows every server you can book, with its lab, GPU model, number of GPUs, and memory per GPU. Filter by GPU model, minimum memory per GPU, minimum number of GPUs, or lab. A server page shows its full specifications and a timeline of free GPUs for the next 7 days, in the lab's time zone. Click a free slot on the timeline to start a booking at that time.

### Finding a slot

If you know what your job needs but not where or when to run it, use "Find a slot". Enter the number of GPUs, the minimum memory per GPU or a GPU model, how long the job runs, and the earliest start and latest end. The results list times on every server that pass all the booking rules for you: the peak-hours limit, your remaining quota, and the server's limits. Click a result to open the booking dialog with it filled in.

## Making your first booking

In the booking dialog, check the server, the number of GPUs, and the start and end times, and describe your job in the purpose field. Times are shown in the lab's time zone. If the booking breaks a rule, the dialog explains which one. On most servers the booking is confirmed immediately; on restricted servers it waits for an administrator.

## After you book

Your booking appears in My reservations and on the dashboard, with the GPU numbers assigned to you. Connect to the server at the start time and use only the GPUs you were given.

### Adding to your calendar

Click "Add to calendar" on a reservation to download a calendar file and import it into your calendar app. You also receive an email reminder about an hour before the booking starts.

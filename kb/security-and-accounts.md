# Security and accounts

## One account per person

Each person uses their own account, registered with their own email address. Do not share accounts or passwords, and do not book on someone else's behalf with their account. Quotas, reservations, and the audit log are tied to the account, so sharing one makes your group's usage impossible to tell apart and breaks the fair-use limits.

New accounts are student accounts. Administrator rights are granted only by the lab administrators.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 8 characters long, and up to 128. A long passphrase is better than a short complex password. Do not reuse a password from another service. Change your password from your profile page; you need your current password to do it.

Changing your password signs out every other device where you were signed in.

### Sign-in limits

To protect accounts from password guessing, sign-in attempts are limited. After 5 failed sign-ins for the same email address within 15 minutes, further attempts for that address are refused until the 15-minute window ends, so you may have to wait up to 15 minutes before trying again. A successful sign-in clears the count. There is also a high limit per network address.

Wrong current passwords when changing your password are limited the same way: 5 per 15 minutes.

## Sessions and signing out

You stay signed in on the same browser for up to 7 days after you last used the app there. Signing out ends the session on that browser. On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you are done.

If you think someone else has used your account, change your password, which ends every other session, and tell the lab administrators.

## Account deactivation

Lab administrators can deactivate an account, for example when someone leaves the university. A deactivated account cannot sign in. Deactivation also releases all of the account's reservations at once: pending requests and approved bookings that have not started are cancelled, and running bookings are ended early. The owner receives an email for each released booking. Reactivating the account does not restore those reservations.

## Reporting a security problem

Report suspected security problems, such as a compromised account, a server running unexpected processes, or a bug in the booking system, to the lab administrators right away. Do not test other users' accounts or data to confirm a problem.

# Frequently asked questions

## Booking

**Can I book GPUs on two servers in one booking?** No. A booking is for one server. Book each server separately; every booking counts toward your active reservations.

**Why can't I find a server my lab mate mentioned?** It may be retired or in an inactive lab. Retired servers are hidden from students.

**Can I change the time of a booking?** Not directly. Cancel it (before it starts) and book the new time.

**The slot I picked from "Find a slot" was refused. Why?** Search results are suggestions. Someone else may have booked the GPUs between your search and your booking. Search again.

See the booking rules for the full list of limits.

## Quota and peak hours

**Do I need to do anything to get my quota each week?** No. Usage is recalculated continuously; each new week starts empty.

**Does an unused quota roll over?** No. Hours you do not use in a week are not carried into the next one.

**Is there a separate limit for evenings?** No. Outside weekday peak hours only your weekly quota and the server's maximum booking length apply.

See the fair use and quota policy and the peak hours page for details and examples.

## Approvals

**How long does approval take?** Administrators review the queue during working days. Request well ahead of the start; an undecided request expires at its start time.

**Can I use an H100 server without approval?** No. Every booking on a restricted server needs approval.

See the approval policy.

## Cancelling

**Is there a penalty for cancelling?** No. Cancel as soon as you know you will not use the time.

**I cancelled a running booking but it shows as completed.** That is expected. Ending a running booking early shortens it and marks it completed, because it did run.

See the cancellation and early release page.

## Hardware

**Which GPU should I pick for my first experiments?** The smallest one that fits, usually an RTX 4090 for small models. See the hardware guide for memory estimates.

**Can I run Docker or install drivers?** Driver and system changes are done only by the administrators. Ask them if your job needs something that is not installed.

## Accounts

**I forgot my password.** There is no self-service password reset yet. Contact the lab administrators from your registered email address.

**I'm leaving the university. What happens to my bookings?** When your account is deactivated, your future bookings are cancelled and running ones are ended. Copy your data off the servers first.
